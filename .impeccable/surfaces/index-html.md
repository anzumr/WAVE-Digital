# Home page (index.html), with the Stripe thank-you page (thank-you.html)

Scope: the whole marketing home page and its thank-you page; a redesign that replaces the visual world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: small-business owners (restaurants and food, local shops and services, startups and solo founders) deciding, on a phone or a Windows laptop, whether WAVE Digital should run their website, social media and ads.

Action: subscribe or pay through Stripe Payment Links, send an inquiry (Formspree), or book a free consultation (Calendly, Google Meet).

Proof and content: the published prices; two real client websites (Ensō no Sato, No.1 Halal Kitchen); the mission statement. No testimonials, metrics, contacts or team details exist; none may be invented.

Constraints: the pinned WebGL wave hero (`wave-bg.js`) and the whole wave-hand logo stay; every price, plan name, fee note and badge stays verbatim; static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, classic scripts, works from `file://`; scrolling must stay smooth on an ordinary Windows laptop in Chrome (no backdrop blur, no infinite decorative loops, no box-shadow or filter animation; motion never costs scrolling).

Memorable moment: plans arrive as order tickets on a steel rail, and firing one starts the order.

Unresolved: the city or region to name for local search.

## Direction contract

THESIS: WAVE's whole offer, made to order. The page is a fine-dining kitchen pass where every plan hangs as an itemised, priced order ticket on a steel rail and is fired in one move. It refuses the category-default agency landing: glowing gradients, a headline over two buttons, rows of same-size icon cards, a "most popular" pricing table.

OWN-WORLD: deep harbour-navy ground continuous with the pinned wave hero; a brushed blue-grey steel rail and pass edge, matte, never chrome gloss; heavyweight white cotton tickets with crisp navy print, perforated tear edges, tabular figures, ticket numbers and fire marks; one cyan accent from the logo, reserved for actions and the fired stamp; a precise text grotesk and a condensed numeric face for prices and ticket numbers. Diner material is translated to fine dining: thermal paper becomes cotton card, red stamps become the brand cyan. No glows, glass, gradient text or eyebrow labels.

STORY: the owner meets the wave and the promise, reads the plans as orders they understand at a glance, sees two real orders served (the client sites, live), then fires a plan, writes an order (inquiry) or reserves a consult. Signature interaction: the plan tickets slide onto the rail once as the rates arrive; hover or focus lifts a ticket off the rail; "Fire" stamps it and opens checkout, or the order pad when checkout is not set up. Motion grammar: one orchestrated arrival, transform and opacity only, exponential ease-out from a visible default.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the pinned wave hero, behaviour unchanged: the logo rises from the water on the right; on the left the headline "Make your business impossible to ignore." at display scale, its supporting line, the primary action to the plans and a secondary action to book a free consult; along the bottom edge the steel rail begins, with the tops of the first tickets showing.
